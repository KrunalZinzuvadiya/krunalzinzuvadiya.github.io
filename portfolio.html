ABOUT ME

Hello! I'm Krunal Zinzuvadiya, currently residing in Pune, India. I am an aspiring data scientist with a solid foundation in engineering and a deep passion for leveraging data to drive insights and innovation.

I completed my schooling with distinction from Ahmedabad, Gujarat. I hold a Bachelor's degree in Engineering from L.D. College of Engineering, Ahmedabad. Currently, I am pursuing my Master's in Data Science and Spatial Analytics at Symbiosis International University, Pune, expected to graduate in 2025.

My key projects include developing a QR Code Scanner using Python and OpenCV, a reinforcement learning bot for Connect Four, a diabetes prediction model using machine learning, and an AutoML tool that automates the entire ML pipeline through a user-friendly Streamlit web app.

In my free time, I enjoy participating in outdoor activities like trekking, and I have completed advanced courses at the Swami Vivekananda Mountaineering Institute. These experiences have taught me valuable lessons in leadership, teamwork, and perseverance.

I am always eager to connect with others in the field of data science and explore opportunities to contribute to exciting projects while continuing my journey of personal and professional growth.
